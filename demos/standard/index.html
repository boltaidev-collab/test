Notes to the summary

Revenue and profit are for continuing operations. Underlying measures exclude exceptional items. In 2025 the only exceptional item was £4.2 million relating to the closure of the Sheffield depot, which also took £16.8 million of revenue out of Workplace Services.

This digital summary is prepared from management figures. It is not a substitute for the statutory annual report and accounts.

Building Systems contributed £412.4 million, Water Infrastructure £267.8 million, and Workplace Services £162.4 million. The United Kingdom remained 71 percent of group revenue.
